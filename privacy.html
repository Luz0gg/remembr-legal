Last updated: 7 September 2026 Effective date: 18 July 2026 (to be adjusted to the actual store publication date)

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The Remembr application (the "App", "we", "us") is published by:

  • Publisher / data controller: Tom Ravinet — private individual
  • Contact (personal data): remembr.apps@gmail.com

Remembr is a personal project offered free of charge, outside any professional activity.

The data controller is the person who decides why and how your personal data is used. For any question about this policy or your rights, contact us at the address above.

This policy applies in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable French data protection law.

2. What the app does

Remembr is a geolocated visual archive: you take or import photos, which are placed on a world map based on their location. The app also offers social features (friends, map sharing, public profiles, messaging) and gamification (levels, badges…).

An account is required to use the app.

3. What data do we collect?

3.1 Account data

  • E-mail address (account creation, sign-in, password reset).
  • Password (stored encrypted/hashed by our authentication provider; we never have access to it in clear text).
  • Unique public username (@handle), chosen at sign-up.
  • If you use "Continue with Google": your Google account identifier and verified e-mail (provided by Google to authenticate you). We do not receive your Google password.

3.2 Profile data

  • Display name, biography, profile picture (avatar) — which you provide freely.
  • App preferences (theme, settings, language, profile privacy).

3.3 Content you create (photos, notes, audio)

  • Photos you take or import, and their metadata: GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude), capture date, optional written note.
  • Location data derived from your photos (country, city) obtained via reverse geocoding.
  • Voice notes (audio memos) attached to a photo, if you record any.
Images and voice notes are stored in a private storage space, partitioned per account: by default, only you can access them (see §5 for social sharing).

3.4 Social data

  • Your friends list, your friend requests, the accounts you have blocked.
  • Your map sharing settings (who you share with, which trips you hide).
  • The messages you send and receive (private and group conversations).
  • The reactions and comments you leave on shared photos.

3.5 Technical data

  • Your device's notification token (to send you push notifications), if you enable them.
  • Minimal technical data required for operation and security (e.g. synchronization timestamps).

3.6 Reports and moderation

  • If you report content: the content type, its reference, and the optional reason.
  • Our moderation logs (actions performed by the administrator) — see §9.

We deliberately do not collect: any banking data, any biometric data, and any "sensitive data" within the meaning of the GDPR (opinions, health, etc.). Please do not publish any such data.

5. Who can see your content? (social sharing)

By default, your photos and trips are private — visible only to you. They become visible to others only if you explicitly enable it:

  • Map sharing with a friend: you choose, friend by friend, to show them your map. Sharing is directional (you can see theirs without them seeing yours, and vice versa).
  • Profile privacy: "private" (default), "friends", or "public". It determines who can see your statistics and your trip showcase.
  • Messages: visible only to members of the conversation.
  • Reactions / comments: visible to people who have access to the relevant photo.

You can hide trips, define private zones (e.g. your home, which never appears on public surfaces), and block a user at any time.

6. Who do we share data with? (processors and third parties)

We do not sell your data. We rely on technical providers ("processors") strictly necessary for operation:

  • Supabase — hosting of the database, authentication, file storage (photos, audio, avatars), and server functions. Hosting region: European Union (Ireland).
  • Google — - Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with Google. - Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): technical delivery of push notifications to your device. - Google Maps: displaying the map background.
  • Expo — technical push-notification delivery service (relay to FCM).

These providers act only on our instructions and are not authorized to use your data for their own purposes. We may also disclose data where required by law (court order, etc.).

7. Transfers outside the European Union

Your data hosted by Supabase is stored within the European Union (Ireland).

However, some providers such as Google (Google Sign-In, push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Maps) and Expo may process certain data outside the EU, in particular in the United States. Such transfers are governed by appropriate safeguards provided for by the GDPR, in particular the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long do we keep your data?

  • Account data and content: kept for as long as your account is active.
  • Account deletion: when you delete your account from the app, your personal data and files are erased from our servers (see §10). Exception: messages you have sent to others may be kept in their conversations, but anonymized (shown as "Deleted account"), so as not to alter other people's exchanges.
  • Moderation logs: kept for 12 months, then deleted.
  • Reported content: when a message is reported, its text is kept aside while the report is being reviewed — including if its author deletes it in the meantime, without which the person who reported it would be left with nothing to show. That copy is accessible to the administrator only, appears in no conversation, and is deleted 90 days after the report has been handled.
  • Banned account: content is erased 30 days after the ban. The e-mail address and the @ handle are then kept with no time limit, as an empty record, to prevent the sanction from being evaded (§9).
  • Reported-message evidence and account deletion: if you delete your account while one of your messages has been reported, the copy of that message kept for review (see above) may remain for up to 90 days, by then unlinked from your identity (the sender is cleared from it).
  • Technical backups: purged according to our host's cycles.

9. Content moderation

Because the app lets you share content and interact with other users, we operate moderation, for the purpose of safety and legal compliance (not surveillance).

Who moderates. One person: the app's publisher. There is no moderation team, no subcontractor, and no automated scanning of your content.

What the administrator can access. We would rather state this plainly than imply otherwise:

  • Photos, trips, profiles and files (originals, voice notes, stories): the administrator can view them, including without a prior report. This is what the administrator role of an image-hosting service entails: being able to check an account reported for something else, to act on manifestly illegal content, or to respond to a lawful request. It is neither automated, nor systematic, nor used for any purpose other than moderation.
  • Private messages: this is the exception. They are accessible only while an open report specifically targets them. Outside that case, the content of your conversations cannot be viewed.

What is recorded. Every moderation action (warning, freeze, ban, content removal, report dismissal) is logged: who acted, on what, why, and when. That log is kept for 12 months (§8).

Possible sanctions.

  • Warning: a message, with no effect on the account.
  • Freeze: the account can still be viewed but can no longer publish or write. Reversible.
  • Ban: access to the app is blocked. Your content is erased after 30 days; your e-mail address and your @ handle are then kept with no time limit, as an empty record, so that the sanction cannot be evaded by creating a new account. A banned account cannot be deleted while the sanction is in force.

You can challenge a sanction from within the app (appeal form) or by e-mail.

This mechanism relies on our legitimate interest in ensuring a safe service and on our legal obligation to promptly remove manifestly illegal content reported to us.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access: obtain a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data (some of it can be edited directly in the app).
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"): you can delete your account directly from the app (Settings), which erases your data and files from our servers.
  • Portability: retrieve your content (the app offers a gallery export).
  • Restriction and objection to processing, in the cases provided for by law.
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time (e.g. notifications).

To exercise these rights, contact us at remembr.apps@gmail.com. We respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL — www.cnil.fr) if you believe your rights are not being respected.

11. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures: per-account data partitioning, access rules enforced server-side (a user can only access their own data or what is explicitly shared with them), private storage of files, encrypted connections. As no system is infallible, we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we strive to protect your data to the best of the current state of the art.

12. Children and minimum age

13. Push notifications

14. Trackers / cookies
