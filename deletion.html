Delete your Remembr account

App concerned: Remembr (com.worldmemorymap.app), published by Tom Ravinet. Last updated: 7 September 2026

You can delete your account and your data at any time, through either of two routes.

1. From inside the app (immediate)

This is the fastest route, and it involves no waiting.

  1. Open Remembr and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → My account.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm both warnings.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

2. By e-mail, without installing the app

If you no longer have the app on your phone, or cannot access it, write to:

remembr.apps@gmail.com

Include in your message:

  • the e-mail address of your Remembr account, or your @ handle;
  • the subject line: "Account deletion".

We verify that the request comes from the account holder, then carry out the deletion. We reply within 30 days at the latest, and in practice much sooner.

3. What gets deleted

Everything that belongs to you, on our servers:

  • your account and its sign-in identifier;
  • your profile: name, biography, profile picture, @ handle, preferences;
  • all the information attached to your photos: location, date, note, country, city, favourites;
  • all your files in our storage: original photos, sharing variants, voice notes, stories, profile picture;
  • your friendships, blocks, shared maps, reactions and comments;
  • your progress: level, badges, coins, transaction history;
  • your notification tokens and your reports.

The photos in your phone's own gallery are never touched: Remembr neither modifies nor deletes them, before or after account deletion.

4. What is kept, and why

Two limited exceptions:

  • Messages you have sent to other people remain in their conversations, but anonymised: your name is replaced by "Deleted account" and the link to your account is severed. Without this, deleting an account would punch holes in other people's conversations, and they are equally entitled to keep their own exchanges.
  • A copy of a reported message, if one of your messages was the subject of a report under review, may remain for up to 90 days, unlinked from your identity (the sender is cleared from it). This is what allows a report to be handled through to the end.

Our host's technical backups follow their own purge cycles.

5. One special case: an account under sanction

If your account is currently banned, deletion is refused while the sanction is in force. Without that rule, deleting your account would be enough to erase a sanction and immediately create a new one. Your content is then erased after 30 days, and only your e-mail address and @ handle remain reserved.

You can challenge a sanction from within the app, or at the address above.

6. Questions

For any question about your data: remembr.apps@gmail.com.

Full details of what we collect and keep are in our privacy policy.
